---

## 🛠 Core Technical Stack

We leverage a modern, performance-oriented stack to deliver robust software solutions. Our architectural decisions are guided by the principles of **scalability**, **telemetry-first monitoring**, and **automated resilience**.

* **Languages & Frameworks:** Expert-level development in **C#** and the **.NET LTS** ecosystem, specializing in asynchronous backend services and distributed systems.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Deep integration with **Azure** (App Services, Functions, Service Bus) and **Cloudflare** (Workers, Pages, R2) for global edge delivery.
* **Containerization & Orchestration:** Standardizing environments through **Docker** to ensure consistency from local development to production-grade deployments.
* **DevOps & Automation:** Implementation of mature CI/CD pipelines via **GitHub Actions**, with a focus on Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and automated security scanning.

---

## 🏛 Engineering Philosophy

At AgeeBgee Solutions, we don't just write code; we engineer systems.

* **Stoic Engineering:** We embrace "voluntary discomfort" in development—investing heavily in testing and edge-case handling early to ensure peace of mind during production.
* **Observability by Default:** We believe if a system isn't measured, it isn't managed. We prioritize OpenTelemetry and integrated logging in every build.
* **Scalable Simplicity:** We strive for the simplest architecture that solves the problem, ensuring that the systems we build today are maintainable by the teams of tomorrow.
